# Ecological Field Survey SWMS Hazard Assurance Review

**Document reviewed:** EC-GEN-SWMS-001, revised to **1.1**  
**Review scope:** The eight supplied Ecology Consulting controlled SWMS modules and the staff-portal daily selector.  
**Review purpose:** To confirm that the generic ecological field survey SWMS identifies each high-risk hazard trigger, preserves the source module’s critical safety boundaries, and requires staff to open and brief the controlling source document.

## Conclusion

**The consolidated Generic SWMS now covers all eight high-risk hazard categories represented in the supplied source PDFs.** Revision 1.1 corrected the material gaps found in the initial consolidation. When staff select a hazard, the portal now displays the source-derived scope limits, pre-start gates, operating controls, linked documents, emergency boundaries and stop-work triggers. It also preserves the key cross-module combinations: falls with water; tunnel with confined space; electrical exposure with plant or traffic; and plant exposure with traffic or electrical/service risk.

The generic document is appropriate as a **daily ecological-survey selector and briefing record**. It is not, and must not be used as, a standalone SWMS for the selected high-risk activity. Each current source PDF, project-specific permit, Principal Contractor control and site Emergency Response Plan remains controlling. This boundary is essential because the source documents contain detailed record-retention, competency, equipment, site-control and emergency-response provisions that cannot safely be replaced by a short electronic selector.

Safe Work Australia identifies all eight reviewed categories—falls above two metres, asbestos disturbance, confined spaces/tunnels, energised services, live traffic corridors, powered mobile plant and water with drowning risk—as high-risk construction work categories when they arise in construction work. [9]

## Material assurance changes implemented

The first change is structural. A selected hazard no longer presents only a short list of controls and stop-work triggers. It now presents five distinct source-derived areas: **scope and non-authorising limits**, **pre-start checks and prerequisites**, **controls to brief and apply**, **required linked controls and documents**, and **emergency boundaries**. The Field Lead must confirm both this content and the stop-work arrangements before the daily SWMS can be submitted.

The second change is cross-module assurance. The generic SWMS now explicitly requires staff to select all linked source modules rather than treating one selection as sufficient. This is particularly important where a creek bank creates a fall and immersion hazard, a tunnel is or may be a confined space, electrical assets are adjacent to plant or traffic, or a clearing operation contains both plant and traffic exposure.

The third change is emergency integrity. The generic SWMS now makes clear that staff must not move an injured person except where immediate danger exists. It prohibits Ecology Consulting staff from attempting confined-space or tunnel rescue, limits water rescue to reach-or-throw unless a person is trained and authorised, prevents electrical-contact approach before the area is confirmed de-energised, and requires plant isolation before responder approach to a crush or entrapment event.

## Residual limitations and required operating discipline

The selector does **not** grant permission for an excluded activity. A fall-risk selection does not authorise scaffolding, an EWP, rope access or abseiling. A confined-space or tunnel selection does not authorise entry without the Principal Contractor’s permit process. Electrical, rail, traffic-control, excavation, plant-operation, asbestos-removal, diving and technical-rescue activities remain outside the authority of this generic form unless a separate controlled arrangement expressly authorises them.

The Field Lead must open the source PDF for each selected module at the work location. The source version, site-specific permits, contractor plans, competency evidence, records, equipment inspections and any source-mandated retention or review requirements remain required. The portal record demonstrates hazard selection and daily briefing; it does not substitute for those source records.

Safe Work Australia’s confined-space guidance reinforces this boundary by requiring a risk assessment, entry permit, continuous external communication and standby monitoring before entry. [10] Its asbestos guidance reinforces the need for an accessible register, management plan and controls that prevent fibre exposure. [11] Its plant guidance reinforces the duty to manage plant risk, maintain plant and provide appropriate training and supervision. [12] SafeWork NSW’s fall guidance reinforces using higher-order controls before fall arrest and having tested emergency/rescue procedures when fall arrest is used. [13]

## Verification completed

The updated selector, staff form, controlled template and regression contract were checked for syntax and required content. The contract now verifies the source-module availability, revision marker, non-authorising limits, prerequisites, linked documents, emergency boundaries, water rescue-rope requirement, plant crush-response boundary, service-location safeguard and electrical boundary.
