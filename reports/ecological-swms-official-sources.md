# Official guidance used for the ecological SWMS assurance review

The supplied Ecology Consulting controlled SWMS PDFs remain the primary source for the internal control wording and activity boundaries. The following regulator pages were used only as a secondary check that the consolidated selector continues to preserve, rather than dilute, the relevant high-risk work controls.

Safe Work Australia identifies work involving a fall of more than two metres, asbestos disturbance, confined spaces or tunnels, energised electrical installations or services, live traffic corridors, powered mobile plant, and water with a drowning risk as high-risk construction work categories. It states that the PCBU must prepare, keep, comply with and review a SWMS for applicable high-risk construction work and provide it to the Principal Contractor. [1]

Safe Work Australia describes confined-space risks, entry-permit requirements, continuous outside communication, standby monitoring, rescue arrangements and relevant retention requirements. [2]

Safe Work Australia describes asbestos registers, asbestos management plans, clear identification or warning information, and the need to manage the risks of airborne fibre exposure. [3]

Safe Work Australia describes plant risks and the PCBU duties to manage plant risk, maintain plant, provide training and supervision, and control unauthorised interference. [4]

SafeWork NSW confirms the hierarchy of controls for falls. It notes that fall-arrest systems are lower in the hierarchy and require emergency and rescue procedures that are tested for effectiveness. [5]
