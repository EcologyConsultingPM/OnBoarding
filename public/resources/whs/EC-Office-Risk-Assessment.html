3. Hierarchy of Controls Reference
How to completeWhen an item is non-compliant, choose corrective controls from the top of the hierarchy down — eliminate before relying on PPE. Reduce risk to ALARP.
WHS Act 2011 s.17 · ISO 45001 Clause 8.1.2
1 — ELIMINATIONMost effective
Remove the hazard entirely. Most effective; consider first.
2 — SUBSTITUTION
Replace the hazard with a safer alternative.
3 — ISOLATION
Separate people from the hazard — distance, barriers, restricted access.
4 — ENGINEERING
Physical / mechanical measures — guards, ventilation, interlocks.
5 — ADMINISTRATIVE
Procedures, training, supervision, signage. Rely on compliance.
6 — PPELeast effective
Last resort. Never the sole control; support with higher controls.
