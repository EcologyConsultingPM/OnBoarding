## Purpose, source hierarchy and limits

This controlled generic SWMS provides a **daily selection and briefing framework** for ecological field surveys. Staff must complete the project details, activity description, site conditions, emergency arrangements and team acknowledgement. They must then select every relevant specialist hazard module, open its current controlled source SWMS and apply the source requirements alongside this document.

> This form is **not a replacement** for a selected source SWMS, the site-specific Emergency Response Plan, a Principal Contractor control, a permit, induction, Traffic Management Plan, Traffic Guidance Scheme, rail-access authority or another required authorisation. It does not authorise construction, excavation or ground disturbance, plant operation, traffic control, electrical work, asbestos disturbance, confined-space or tunnel rescue, diving or other excluded work.

Where a hazard cannot be eliminated or adequately controlled, work must not proceed. Every worker may stop work. The Field Lead must move the team to a safe location, notify the Principal Contractor or site supervisor where applicable, and re-brief controls before recommencement.

The selected source SWMS remains the controlling document for its scope, detailed control wording, mandatory records, training, PPE, cross-references, emergency response and document-retention requirements. The staff portal selector is a briefing aid and a daily evidence record; it must never be treated as a standalone authority for a high-risk activity.

## Daily completion requirements

The staff portal form records the project, client, job number, date, exact survey activity, site, approved access and egress route, Field Lead, Principal Contractor or site supervisor, first aider, communications/standby roles, Emergency Response Plan reference, muster point, coordinates, nearest responder access and check-in arrangement.

The Field Lead must complete a daily go/no-go review of weather, light and visibility, ground/edge/bank stability, water conditions, air/ventilation where relevant, traffic and plant operations, electrical assets, asbestos information, permits, authorisations and communications. Each crew member is listed with a role and acknowledges the daily briefing. The Field Lead confirms that generic controls, selected source documents, source-specific limits, prerequisites, controls, linked documents, emergency boundaries and stop-work triggers were briefed before signing.

## Generic controls applying to all selected work

1. Limit work to ecological survey and observation within the approved project scope. Use remote observation, existing information, cameras, binoculars, drones, torches, borescopes, extendable equipment or another safer method wherever practicable.
2. Before mobilisation and again at the Daily Toolbox Talk, confirm the work area, access and egress, roles, ERP, communications/check-ins, current source SWMS, relevant permits, site controls, task equipment and emergency equipment.
3. Confirm that every worker is fit, competent, trained and authorised for the selected work. Verify current required credentials, inductions, selected PPE, first-aid, communications, monitoring and rescue equipment. Tag out faulty equipment and do not use it.
4. Maintain the required buddy, standby, visual contact, check-in, separation and supervision arrangements. Reassess and re-brief if weather, location, operations, site controls or the survey method changes.
5. Stop work, move to the nominated safe location or muster point, notify the Field Lead and Principal Contractor, and do not recommence until the risk is controlled and communicated.
6. In an emergency, use the site-specific ERP. Make the area safe for responders and **do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger**. Call 000 or 112, or activate InReach or PLB where required. Provide location and access information, account for personnel, report the event, review the source SWMS and ERP, and restock used equipment.

## Assured specialist hazard selector

| Select when this applies | Controlled module | Required daily assurance points |
|---|---|---|
| The survey is near a drop-off, elevated or unstable edge, culvert, bridge, embankment, batter, cliff line, creek bank or retaining structure with a fall risk | **EC-SWMS01 — Fall risk over two metres** | Survey/observe only; no scaffolding, EWP, fall-protection-system alteration, rope access or abseiling. Use safe ground, setbacks, no-go zones and safe access. Check firm/level vehicle parking, weather, visibility, edge stability, buddy/contact and fatigue. Fall arrest is allowed only with Principal Contractor verified engineer-rated anchorage, current Working at Heights competency and inspected equipment. Select EC-SWMS18, buoyancy aid and floating rescue rope if immersion risk applies. |
| Asbestos or asbestos-containing material may be present in structures, soil or fill | **EC-SWMS04 — Asbestos observation only** | Observation only; no disturbance, handling, removal, excavation, raking, lifting or soil/fill disturbance. Review current asbestos register and management plan, obtain written Principal Contractor scope confirmation, follow exclusion zones/signage, avoid removal work and dust, and apply site decontamination. |
| The survey is in or near a culvert, stormwater drain, bridge void, underpass cavity, pit, pipe or enclosed waterway | **EC-SWMS06 — Confined space** | Treat uncertainty as confined space. Consider non-entry first. Entry requires the current Principal Contractor permit, risk assessment, atmospheric test/continuous monitoring, ventilation, isolation, retrieval, entry tracking, dedicated standby and continuous communication. Ecology Consulting personnel do not undertake confined-space rescue. |
| Work is in, at or near a tunnel, culvert or underpass | **EC-SWMS08 — Tunnel ecological survey activities** | Ecological observation/survey only; no construction or interference with plant, services, structure or isolation. Confirm classification, entry permission, go/no-go assessment, atmosphere, water, structure, lighting, communications, emergency exits/muster point and lock-out. Use an outside communications officer, redundant communications and two lights per person. Select EC-SWMS06 if confined or uncertain. |
| Work is on or near energised overhead or underground electrical assets or services | **EC-SWMS12 — Energised electrical installations or services** | No electrical work, excavation in service corridors, No-Go Zone entry or approach to vegetation contacting powerlines. Verify service locations and applicable approach distances, complete electrical induction, mark zones and use remote observation. Keep long equipment clear and horizontal. Select EC-SWMS16/15 where plant/traffic is present. |
| Work is on, in or adjacent to a road, rail or other live traffic corridor | **EC-SWMS15 — Live traffic corridor** | No unauthorised traffic control, plant operation or interference with traffic controls. Verify Principal Contractor approval, TMP/TGS, work zone, buffers, designated parking/access, communication and retreat. For rail, verify written authority, induction, authorised access, lookout/rail safety controls, crossings and continuous rail communication. |
| Powered mobile plant is operating or may move near the ecological team | **EC-SWMS16 — Powered mobile plant** | Ecology staff are observers only: no plant operation, excavation, ground disturbance or construction direction unless separately authorised. If people and plant cannot be separated, do not proceed. Confirm marked zones, barriers, access paths, operator acknowledgement, line of sight, spotter safeguards, BYDA/service controls, inspection/tag-out, plant/traffic inductions and safe parking. Fauna rescue is ground-based only after direct isolation confirmation; use one-tree-length clearing exclusion and all-clear before restart. |
| Work is in or near water or another liquid with a drowning, immersion or unstable-footing risk | **EC-SWMS18 — Water and drowning risk** | No diving, unsafe water, uncontrolled rescue, fast flow, flood water, unknown depth or entry above waist depth unless specifically assessed/controlled. Assess depth/flow/bank/substrate/egress. Confirm buddy and land observer, weather/upstream/tide/release conditions, PFD, gripped footwear, a floating rescue rope of at least 20 m, reaching aid and waterproof/redundant communications. Use reach-or-throw rescue only unless trained and authorised. |

## Source-specific record, cross-reference and stop-work requirements

For every selected module, the Field Lead must record **why the module applies**, the exact task and location, source document revision, and confirmation that its source-specific limits, prerequisites, controls, linked plans/documents, authorisations, training, PPE, equipment, emergency boundaries and stop-work triggers have been briefed.

Where more than one trigger applies, staff must select and apply each linked module. Key combinations are EC-SWMS01 with EC-SWMS18 for immersion; EC-SWMS08 with EC-SWMS06 for a tunnel/culvert that is confined or uncertain; EC-SWMS12 with EC-SWMS16/15 for electrical work near plant/traffic; and EC-SWMS16 with EC-SWMS15/12 where the plant area creates traffic or electrical/service exposure.

Work must stop immediately if a selected module identifies a trigger, including unstable ground or edges; unsafe water, weather, visibility or fatigue; failed atmosphere, ventilation, permit, lighting or communication; unclear services, asset locations or exclusion zones; failed traffic/rail/plant separation; suspected asbestos; tunnel instability; or any worker feeling unsafe. Move to the selected module’s safe location, retreat point or muster point. Do not restart until the source-required control and communication are complete; obtain written clearance where the source requires it.

For an emergency, apply the site-specific ERP and the selected source module. Ecology Consulting personnel do not attempt confined-space or tunnel rescue. Water rescue is reach-or-throw only unless a person is trained and authorised. For electrical contact, remain clear until the area is confirmed de-energised and safe. For a person struck or trapped by plant, do not move the plant unless directed by the operator and it is safe; isolate nearby plant before responders approach.

## Field Lead verification

The Field Lead signs the completed electronic form to confirm that the team has been briefed, current controlled source documents are available, all relevant hazards have been selected, project-specific controls and source prerequisites are in place, emergency arrangements are understood and the activity is authorised to proceed only within the stated limits.
