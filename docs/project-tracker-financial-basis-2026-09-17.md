# Project Tracker financial basis

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026
**Applies to:** Active Project Tracker and Project Health Report

The portal uses the following two intentionally separate financial measures. **Remaining budget** is the accepted project budget less actual recorded time charged at the applicable quoted role rate. **Estimated profitability** is the accepted project budget less the delivery cost, where delivery cost is 60% of the applicable quoted role rate. This is the agreed “minus 40%” cost basis.

> Estimated profitability = accepted budget − (recorded quote-rate spend × 60%)

> Remaining budget = accepted budget − recorded quote-rate spend

The active tracker only includes approved budget sources and active allocations. Closed historical setup sources are retained for audit visibility but do not affect live remaining-budget, utilisation, health, or profitability figures.
